# Ironcoach

A personal gym trainer app: goal-based weekly plan (set up for GymNation Motor City machines), animated form guides for 51 exercises, set logging with rest timer, recovery and readiness tracking, nutrition targets and progress charts.

## Install on Android (Samsung Galaxy S24 FE)
1. Open the app link in **Chrome** or **Samsung Internet**.
2. Chrome: tap ⋮ → **Add to home screen** → **Install**. Samsung Internet: tap ☰ → **Add page to** → **Home screen**.
3. Open Ironcoach from your home screen. It runs full screen and works offline.

Your workouts and meals are stored on your phone only.
The AI photo scanners need the claude.ai version of the app.
